Lowercase italic    & Scalar & $t_\text{dist}$, $\sigma_\text{glob}$, $o_i$, $w$ \\
Bold lowercase      & 3D point (a position in space) & $\mathbf{p}_i$, $\mathbf{q}_j$, $\mathbf{v}_i$ \\
Bold lowercase with an arrow & 3D vector (a direction or displacement) & $\vec{\mathbf{n}}_i$, $\vec{\mathbf{u}}$, $\vec{\mathbf{d}}$, $\vec{\mathbf{t}}$ \\
Bold uppercase      & Matrix & $\mathbf{T}_\text{ses}$, $\mathbf{R}$, $\mathbf{G}$ \\
Uppercase italic    & Any collection or set & $P = \{\mathbf{p}_i\}_{i=1}^{N}$\\
Subscript index     & Running element index & $\mathbf{p}_i$, $g_{ijk}$ \\
Subscript label     & Named role, set in upright text (2--4 letters) & $P_\text{ref}$, $P_\text{ses}$, $\mathbf{T}_\text{img}$ \\
Single prime        & A selected subset of the unprimed quantity & $P'$, $P_\text{ref}'$ \\
Double prime        & A further reduced subset of $P'$ & $P''$ \\
$|\cdot|$           & Cardinality of a set & $|P|$, $|A \cap B|$ \\
$\|\cdot\|$         & Euclidean norm & $\|\mathbf{p}_i - \mathbf{q}_j\|$ \\
$\mathbb{R}^n$      & $n$-dimensional real space & $\mathbf{p}_i \in \mathbb{R}^3$ \\
$\argmin$, $\argmax$ & Argument of the minimum / maximum & $\argmin_{\mathbf{T}} \sum \rho(\cdot)$ \\
